Què NO fem

Serveis de tercers

Perquè l'aplicació funcioni, el teu dispositiu es connecta a:

Firebase és un servei de Google. Encara que el servei el contractem des d'Europa, Google pot tractar aquestes dades en servidors de fora de l'Espai Econòmic Europeu, principalment als Estats Units. Aquestes transferències es fan a l'empara de les clàusules contractuals tipus aprovades per la Comissió Europea. Els altres serveis que hem llistat (OpenStreetMap, Deezer, MusicBrainz, Google Fonts) reben la petició directament del teu dispositiu, i per tant la teva adreça IP, sense que hi intervinguem.

  • Les preferències del telèfon: fins que desinstal·lis l'aplicació o n'esborris les dades.

Pots desactivar les notificacions des de l'aplicació mateixa, i en fer-ho s'esborren del servidor les dades que hi servien. Per esborrar el compte, escriu-nos a l'adreça de dalt: el suprimirem, amb tot el que hi vagi associat, i no cal que ens diguis per què. També pots presentar una reclamació davant l'Autoritat Catalana de Protecció de Dades.

Menors

L'aplicació no s'adreça a menors de 14 anys i no en recull dades conscientment.

Canvis

What we do NOT do

  • We do not track your location in the background. The app does not ask for that permission and holds no record of where you have been.
  • We request precise location, but only while you are using the app and only to work out distances on your phone. With Android's approximate location the position can be a couple of kilometres out — enough to name the wrong town. Android lets you choose Precise or Approximate when it asks, and the app works with either.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • There is no advertising and no ad profiling.
  • There is no behavioural analytics inside the app.

Third-party services

For the app to work, your device connects to:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database and notifications), acting as a data processor.
  • OpenStreetMap, for the map tiles. They receive the request for the tiles you look at, and so which area you are looking at.
  • Deezer and MusicBrainz, when you search for or open an artist. They receive the name searched, never who searched it.
  • Google Fonts, for typography.

If you tap an external link (Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, TikTok, ticket sales…), it opens in your browser and that site's policy applies from then on, not this one.

Where the data is processed

Firebase is a Google service. Even where the service is contracted from Europe, Google may process this data on servers outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Those transfers rely on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. The other services listed (OpenStreetMap, Deezer, MusicBrainz, Google Fonts) receive the request straight from your device, and therefore your IP address, with no involvement from us.

How long we keep it

  • The account: while it is open.
  • Notification data: until you turn notifications off, which deletes the server-side record.
  • On-device preferences: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction or objection, and withdraw consent at any time. You can turn notifications off in the app itself, which erases the data that served them from the server. To delete your account, write to the address above: we will remove it, and everything attached to it, without asking why. You may also lodge a complaint with the Catalan Data Protection Authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you inside the app.
